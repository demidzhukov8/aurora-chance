Aurora Chance — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Aurora Chance ("the app") shows the chance to see the northern lights at your location tonight. The app is published on the App Store by Dmytro Zhukov ("we", "us").

We built the app to need as little data as possible. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers of our own.

2. Service providers

  • MET Norway — cloud forecast (data under CC BY 4.0). Receives rounded coordinates and the app's identification header. api.met.no
  • NOAA SWPC — aurora and Kp data (public). swpc.noaa.gov
  • Apple — App Store purchases and delivery of notifications on your device.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. Privacy policy
  • PostHog (EU cloud) — anonymous usage statistics. Privacy policy
  • Sentry — crash reports. Privacy policy

3. Retention

  • On your phone: until you delete the app.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: kept by PostHog and Sentry for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide the subscription and as required by law.

4. Your rights

You can use the app without sharing your location (choose a city instead), and you can turn off location and notifications at any time in iOS Settings. Because the data we receive is anonymous, we usually cannot link it to you; if you want us to look into anything, contact us at the address below and we will help. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, deletion and objection, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (16 in some EU countries) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
