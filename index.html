Aurora Chance

Your chance to see the northern lights tonight, in one number: aurora activity, clouds, darkness and the Moon for your spot.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Write to demidzhukov8@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Why is the chance low when the aurora forecast looks strong? The number also counts clouds, how dark it is and how bright the Moon is at your spot. A strong aurora behind thick clouds is a low chance.

Do I have to share my location? No. You can pick a city instead. If you share it, coordinates rounded to about 1 km are used only to get the cloud forecast.

How do I cancel a subscription? In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aurora Chance. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone? Open Aurora Pro in the app and tap Restore.
