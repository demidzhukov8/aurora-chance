1. A forecast, not a guarantee

Aurora Chance estimates the chance of seeing the aurora from public space-weather data (NOAA), cloud forecasts (MET Norway) and astronomical calculations. Aurora and weather are hard to predict. A high chance does not guarantee a sighting, and a low chance does not mean the aurora cannot appear. Do not rely on the app for safety, travel or financial decisions.

2. Stay safe outside

Aurora watching usually means cold, dark places. Dress for the conditions, tell someone where you are going, do not stop on roads, respect private land and local rules. You are responsible for your own safety.
