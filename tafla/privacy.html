Tafla — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Tafla ("the app") is a party game with question cards, Impostor and Charades. The app is published on the App Store by Dmytro Zhukov ("we", "us").

There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, the app works offline, and we run no servers of our own.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising and do not combine it with data from other companies.

2. Service providers

  • Apple — App Store purchases, notifications and optional crash reports.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. Privacy policy

3. Retention

  • On your phone: until you delete the app or reset it in Settings.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide the subscription and as required by law.

4. Your rights

Everything you type stays on your phone; deleting the app deletes it. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, deletion and objection, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Write to us at the address below and we will help.

5. Age

Tafla is rated 18+ in the App Store and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
