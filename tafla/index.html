Tafla

Party games in one app: Truth or Dare, Never Have I Ever, Most Likely To, Would You Rather, Get to Know, How Well Do You Know Me?, Roulette, Impostor and Charades. Pass the phone and play.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea for a card? Write to demidzhukov8@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Do cards repeat? No. The app remembers which cards you have seen in each language. When a deck is finished, you can start it again.

What is free? The Classic deck in every game, a few trial cards from every other deck, and two categories in Impostor and Charades. Tafla Pro unlocks all decks and categories.

How do I cancel a subscription? In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tafla. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone? Open Tafla Pro in the app and tap Restore.

How do I change the language? Settings → Language. The interface and the cards switch together.

Why can't I open the Spicy deck? It is for adults only and opens after you confirm you are 18 or older, even with Pro.
